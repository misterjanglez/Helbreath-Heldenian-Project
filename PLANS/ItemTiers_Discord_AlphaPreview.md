# 🗡️ Loot 2.0 — The Tiered Item System (Design Preview)

Nothing is live yet — this is the design locked so far, and **is being developed in a way that if it doesn't work it can be thrown away for HB classic drop rolls.**

## The Idea

Gear now drops in four tiers:
🟢 **Common** — 1 modifier
🔵 **Rare** — 2 modifiers
🟣 **Epic** — 3 modifiers
🟠 **Legendary** — 4 modifiers

That's the whole tier system: **tier = how many modifiers the item has.** Base stats (damage, defense, weight, durability) are identical at every tier — a Legendary sword swings exactly like a Common one. The power is entirely in the modifiers.

## What Drops Tiered

Weapons (**bows included**), body armor, helms, leggings, shields, and capes.
**Not** tiered: named uniques (Xelima, Kloness, Merien), necklaces \& rings, angels, and Dark Items. Those all stay as they are.

## Modifier Categories — The One-Per-Category Rule

Every modifier belongs to a themed category, and an item can only ever roll **one modifier per category**. That's the rule that kills degenerate stacking — no quad-damage swords, no double-crit armor. A Legendary's four modifiers are always four *different kinds* of power.

**⚔️ Weapons (melee \& bows):**

* **Damage** — Sharp, Critical, Poisoning, Righteous, Ancient
* **Precision** — Hitting Probability, Consecutive Attack
* **Handling** — Agile, Light, Strong
* **Economy** — Experience %, Gold %
* **Attributes** — stat bonuses (see the ladder below)
* **Exotic** *(Legendary-only)* — see Legendary Exotics

**🪄 Wands:**

* **Casting** — Spell Success % (the classic "Wizardry" line)
* **Precision**, **Economy**, **Attributes** — same as weapons
* **Exotic** *(Legendary-only)* — cast speed

**🛡️ Armor, Helms, Leggings, Shields, Capes:**

* **Set Line** — the eight classics: Defense Ratio, Physical Absorb, Magic Resist, Magic Absorb, HP/SP/MP Recovery, Poison Resist. **One set line per piece**, so building a full absorb or recovery set is still an across-your-whole-outfit game, just like always.
* **Handling** — Strong, Light
* **Combat Utility** — Mana Converting, Critical Chance
* **Attributes** — the stat ladder
* **Exotic** *(Legendary-only)* — movement speed, on capes \& leggings only

Every modifier combo that exists in the game today stays legal — nothing you know disappears. The new rules just control what can *stack*.

## The Attribute Ladder 📊

Stat bonuses come in three forms (one form per item):

* **Singles** — +N to one stat (STR, DEX, MAG, INT, VIT, CHR). Any tier.
* **Pairs** — *Epic and up only.* Two stats on one line, each rolled separately: STR \& DEX, INT \& MAG, VIT \& STR, VIT \& INT. A "+5 STR \& +4 DEX" line is a real, distinct find.
* **ALL STATS** — *Legendary only.* One number, every stat. The iconic line.

So the moment you see a stat pair, you know it's Epic+. See ALL STATS or an exotic? That's a Legendary, no appraisal needed.

## Legendary Exotics ✨

Each equipment class gets exclusive Legendary-only effects that exist nowhere else:

* **Weapons** — **Sunder** \& **Bleed** on-hit procs (up to 15% chance), or **MP drain** / **SP drain** per hit (burn the enemy's mana or stamina with every connect)
* **Wands** — **cast speed**, up to 20% faster casting 
* **Capes \& Leggings** — **movement speed**, up to 10% per item, hard-capped at 20% total

## How Values Roll 🎲

Here's the part that keeps every drop interesting: **every tier can roll the full value range of any modifier.** A Common can roll a max-value Sharp line — tier changes your *odds* of a high roll, never what's *possible*. Higher tiers are luckier, not exclusive.

* A **perfect roll** on a Legendary line is roughly a **1-in-25,000** event
* A **both-perfect stat pair** is around **1-in-100,000**.
* Old fixed lines get real rolls now: Gold and Experience bonuses roll a range instead of one flat value, and Mana Converting / Crit Chance get clean honest numbers

## Stacking Caps

Modifiers on multiple equipped pieces add up — then hit a hard cap per modifier (for example: Mana Converting caps at 13%, Crit Chance at 20%, move speed at 20%). You can still build around a theme; you just can't break the game with six pieces of the same thing. Angels and unique built-ins don't count against these caps.

## Where Legendary Sits

A god-rolled Legendary is meant to be **the best gear in the game** — beating +15 Dark weapons and top uniques. But uniques keep their signature effects (Xelima's drain, Medusa's petrify, Merien's immunity…) forever — those never appear on dropped gear, so uniques stay special.

## Looking Further Ahead 🔮

None of this is designed yet — no promises on how or when — but the tier system is deliberately built so these can exist later:

* **Tier progression** — a way to grow an item you love: Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary. Since tier is just modifier count, upgrading means earning a new modifier on the item you already have.
* **Modifier re-rolling** — a mechanic to re-roll an *individual* modifier on an item, chasing a better value (or a different line) without throwing the whole item away.
* **Unique forging** — named uniques may even get a path to earn a modifier (or more) of their own — an extremely hard progression that would make your Xelima or Kloness truly one of a kind.

Expect each of these to come with its own difficulty, cost, and risk. The lottery stays a lottery — these are ways to keep playing it with an item you're attached to, not a workshop for free upgrades.
