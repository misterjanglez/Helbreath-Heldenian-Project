# Guild progression burns real player resources; perks concentrate in claimable Title slots

Guild progression (new in Phase 2 — the original had none; design session #93, 2026-08-07) is fed **only by Donations that destroy the donor's resources**: donated gold is burned, donated enemy kills decrement the same EK counter Hero-armor eligibility reads, donated contribution decrements the contribution stat. Burned amounts mint Guild XP → data-driven Guild Levels. The **Treasury** — the guild's stored, withdrawable gold wallet — mints nothing in either direction. The firewall exists because withdrawable gold that earned XP is an infinite pump (deposit → withdraw → redeposit the same coins); and the burn is deliberate design, not anti-abuse alone: sacrificing resources that compete with personal accolades is what makes a member's contribution to the collective mean something.

Guild Levels unlock **Title slots** rather than blanket guild-wide buffs: **Commander** (war duty, consumed by Phase 3), **Huntmaster** (PvE bonuses), **Raidmaster** (PvP bonuses). Slot *counts* scale with Guild Level; claiming is first-come-first-served from the guild UI (Commander claims additionally require Officer+ per ADR 0006); one Title per member; Officers+ may strip/re-assign; an AFK/offline timer auto-releases hogged slots; bonuses apply only while held. Concentrated, rotating, capped power was chosen over passive whole-guild stat buffs because it bounds the balance surface (N titled players, not N×members) and creates an in-guild game of earning the slot. All bonus values, passive-perk selections (member-cap growth is the committed mechanism; repair discount / Heldenian mercenary budget / max-level gold name are candidates; guildhall recall rejected), XP rates, and slot curves ship as neutral data gated behind a dedicated balance design session — the machinery lands with Phase 2, the numbers do not.
